==================================================
PRODUCT
==================================================

CLIPPEX is a premium performance-based creator marketing platform connecting brands and short-form content creators.

Core product loop:

Brand launches campaign
→ creators discover campaigns
→ creators participate
→ creators create and submit content
→ brands/admin review submissions
→ content is published/tracked
→ verified performance is collected
→ creators earn based on verified performance
→ creators withdraw earnings
→ brands track campaign performance

The product should feel like a serious premium SaaS platform, not a generic influencer marketplace.

==================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================

Typography is extremely important.

Use a modern premium sans-serif aesthetic.

Create a strong hierarchy:

Display:
Large, bold, confident headlines.

H1:
Very large and heavy.

H2:
Strong section headings.

H3:
Clear card and page hierarchy.

Body:
Highly readable neutral text.

Metadata:
Smaller muted typography.

Labels:
Small uppercase or compact semibold labels where appropriate.

Use:
- strong weight contrast
- generous line-height
- controlled letter spacing
- short paragraphs
- clear information hierarchy

Do not make everything bold.

Typography should create most of the visual personality.
